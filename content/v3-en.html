How it works:

  • Sub-20 5K and 42:00 10K are the same fitness level (Daniels VDOT about 49 to 50), so one block of training serves both, and the 5K comes first.

  • Until 11 Dec: HYROX + 5K + functional. Thursday trains the race's 8 km of running as 1 km reps between stations; Tuesday trains 5K speed. After the race, no more HYROX stations.

  • Chest and back twice a week (Monday heavy, Friday volume), no bench press, plus pull-ups.

  • There's no full rest day in your week (3 BJJ + 4 gym + 3 runs + swim). That works only if the easy days stay easy, so Wednesday's run is truly easy, Friday can become swim-only when you're tired, and every 3rd to 4th week is lighter.

Goals and starting point

What you told me

  • HYROX: Mixed Doubles, 11 Dec, the only HYROX race this season. You do 60 to 70% of each station, so you train at full race weights. Your partner runs faster, so you set the run pace. Wall balls are your weakest station.

  • Running: endurance type, not a sprinter. One interval session, one easy run of 8 to 12 km and one long run of 15 to 20 km a week. Intervals on weekdays, long runs at the weekend.

  • Gym: 4 visits a week, either 3 normal gym + 1 HYROX, or 2 + 2. Format: 20 min warm-up, 40 min body-part focus, 20 min functional. Legs on any day, Sunday preferred. No bench press, very little landmine work.

  • Body: a stronger and bigger chest and back, an athlete's build, not a bodybuilder's.

  • BJJ: white belt, back from 1 Nov, classes on Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday.

  • Swim: once a week, about 400 m.

  • How to write it: cite Daniels' Running Formula (丹尼尔斯经典跑步训练法) where used, say which parts are my own, explain the theories.

  5. Neutral/V-grip pulldown 3 x 8–10

  6. Dead hang 2 x max

  7. Functional 20 (until 11 Dec): wall-ball block of the week, then 3 x 500 m ski at race effort with 1 min rest. After 11 Dec: carries and KB swings.

Gym C, Friday: chest, back, shoulders, arms, volume. Then swim about 400 m easy (technique, relaxed breathing). If you feel flat on Friday, skip the gym and only swim.

How hard

Work at 1 to 3 reps in reserve (RIR). Add weight when you hit the top of the rep range on every set. Aim for 12 to 16 hard sets per week each for chest and for back, which Gym A plus Gym C gives you. Legs get one main day, because running and HYROX already load them.

Basis: hypertrophy volume and effort guidelines (Schoenfeld and colleagues' volume research; RIR-based training). The weekly layout and exercise choices are my own programming built on your picks.

BJJ: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday

You restart on 2 Nov as a white belt. The plan treats BJJ as skill practice, not as a training load to push. Three practical rules:

  1. Run or lift first, BJJ later. On Monday do Gym A at lunch; on Tuesday and Wednesday run in the morning.

  2. Hard sparring only after the race. Until 11 Dec, drill and do light rolls. In race week (7 to 10 Dec), go once at most, very light, or skip.

  3. Protect the neck, grip and lower back. The neck series and Copenhagen plank in the menu are there for BJJ. Grip is the shared limit between BJJ, pull-ups and the sled pull, so if your forearms are tired after Wednesday, use straps for the rows and pulldowns that week.

Basis: race standards from the HYROX rulebook (doubles weights); split targets are your own; concurrent training ordering from the interference-effect research (do the more important session first, separate by several hours).

Recovery and load

With 3 BJJ classes, 4 gym days, 3 runs and a swim, there is no full rest day. That works only if the easy days are really easy. These are the safety valves:

SignalWhat to do
Garmin HRV below baseline 2 days in a row, or resting HR 5+ bpm above normalMake that day easy: swap intervals for an easy run, or drop the gym part
Tired on FridaySwim only, skip Gym C
Legs heavy on SaturdayCut the long run by 3 to 4 km, keep it easy
Any pain that changes how you runStop the run, replace with bike or swim, tell me
Weekly running load jumpsKeep the acute-to-chronic ratio at 1.0 to 1.3 (Garmin training load)

Lighter weeks: W4 (16 to 22 Nov) and W6 (30 Nov to 6 Dec) before the race, then one in every 3 to 4 weeks after that, with running and gym volume about 30% lower.

Sleep and food: 7.5 to 9 hours of sleep. Protein 110 to 140 g a day (about 1.6 to 2.0 g per kg), spread over 4 meals. Carbs around the interval, long-run and HYROX days. Before Saturday long runs above 15 km, eat breakfast and take 30 to 60 g of carbs per hour once past 75 minutes.

Heart rate: your max HR is 201 bpm and resting about 55. Use Garmin zones based on those numbers. Easy runs should stay in zone 2, under about 150 to 155 bpm.

Basis: training load ratio (Gabbett), lighter weeks every 3 to 4 weeks (classic periodisation), protein targets from ISSN guidelines; HRV-guided adjustment is standard practice.
