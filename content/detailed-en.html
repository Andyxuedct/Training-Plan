The concept

Goal: HYROX Mixed Doubles, Stockholm, Friday 11 Dec. Break 1:15 together, aim for about 1:10. Side goals in the same 7 weeks: get the 5K close to 20:00 (check on 21 Nov), pull-ups from 15 to about 18, keep building chest and back.

How it works, in four ideas:

  1. Running is the engine. HYROX is 8 km of running plus stations. Three runs a week (interval, easy, long) bring your volume back up after the October break, from about 40 km to about 49 km a week. Easy runs are 10 to 12 km, like your usual 10 km runs in September.

  2. One hard HYROX session a week, always at race weight or heavier and at race effort. You can already do the whole race solo, so there is no technique phase. Thursday goes: full solo benchmark (W1), running off the sleds (W2), first half with heavier sleds (W3), second half at race pace (W4), full simulation with your partner (W5), race-pace sharpener (W6).

  3. Gym keeps the strength and the look. Monday heavy chest and back, Friday upper-body volume, Sunday legs, all at 4 hard sets from week 1. Each gym day ends with 20 min of hard functional work (wall-ball blocks, ski intervals, lunges, burpee broad jumps), so you hit stations 3 to 4 times a week. Each gym day also has 15 min of wall-ball strength, plus the movements your videos rate best (★ and 🏅 in the session library).

  4. Two lighter weeks. W4 (16 to 22 Nov) and W6 (30 Nov to 6 Dec) cut volume by about 25 to 30% but keep the intensity, so you arrive at the race fresh.
